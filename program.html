First program

Mat Pilates at home.

The program

  1. 01

    Breathing and core

    Lie on your back, bend your knees, and breathe naturally while gently engaging your core.

    3 min
  2. 02

    Pelvic curls

    Lift and lower your hips slowly. Keep the movement controlled from start to finish.

    5 min
  3. 03

    Single leg stretch

    Move one leg at a time while keeping your shoulders relaxed and your breathing steady.

    6 min
  4. 04

    Side kicks

    Work each side with slow kicks. Choose a range of motion that feels right for your body.

    6 min
  5. 05

    Cool down stretch

    Finish with calm stretching. Take your time and let your breathing slow down.

    5 min

Good to know

Quality over quantity. Keep breathing naturally, move with control, and listen to your body throughout the program.
