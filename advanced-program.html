Advanced program

  1. 01

    The hundred

    Hold a strong curl, pump your arms, and keep the breath steady while your legs stay lifted.

    100 pumps
  2. 02

    Double leg stretch

    Reach arms and legs away from your center, then pull everything back in with control.

    12 reps
  3. 03

    Single straight leg stretch

    Switch straight legs in the air while keeping your chest lifted and your pelvis quiet.

    10 each side
  4. 04

    Criss cross

    Rotate from your ribs toward the bent knee. Keep both elbows wide and move slowly.

    10 each side
  5. 05

    Rollover prep

    Lift your hips from the mat with control, then lower down one vertebra at a time.

    8 reps
  6. 06

    Side plank lift

    Press through your forearm, lift your hips, and hold a long line from head to heels.

    30 sec each side
  7. 07

    Swimming

    Lift opposite arm and leg, then switch with small fast pulses while keeping your neck long.

    45 sec
  8. 08

    Teaser prep

    Balance behind your sit bones, extend one leg, and keep your spine tall as you breathe.

    8 each side
  9. 09

    Plank shoulder taps

    Hold a strong plank and tap opposite shoulders without rocking your hips side to side.

    20 taps
  10. 10

    Advanced flow stretch

    Move from kneeling lunge to hamstring stretch with slow control on both sides.

    60 sec each side

Take your time

This program is stronger and more controlled. Rest when you need to, and return to the first program whenever your form needs a reset.
