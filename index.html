Good to know

  • Holding your breath - Keep breathing naturally throughout each movement
  • Using momentum - let your muscles do the work, not speed
  • Forgetting your core - Think gentle engagement, not maximum tension
  • Ouality over quantity - Good form matters more than more reps
  • Take your time - control is a key part of pilates
  • listen to your body - choose the version that feels right for you
